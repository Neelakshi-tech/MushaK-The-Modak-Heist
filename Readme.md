# 🐭 Modak Heist

A fun, fast-paced game where **Mushak sets out on a mission to steal delicious modaks** while surviving a series of challenging obstacles.

## 🎮 About the Game

**Modak Heist** is a casual challenge-based game inspired by the playful relationship between **Lord Ganesha, Mushak, and modaks**.

The goal is simple:

> **Steal as many modaks as possible and escape without getting caught!**

Players must react quickly, overcome challenges, and make strategic decisions to complete the heist.

## 🕹️ Gameplay

* 🐭 Play as Mushak
* 🍬 Collect/steal modaks
* ⚡ Complete fast-paced challenges
* 🧩 Avoid obstacles and traps
* 🏆 Try to achieve the highest score
* 🔄 Replay to improve your performance

## 🎯 Objective

Complete the Modak Heist while collecting as many modaks as possible.

Your score depends on how successfully you complete the challenges and how many modaks you manage to collect.

## ✨ Features

* Fun and simple gameplay
* Cute Indian festival-inspired theme
* Challenge-based mechanics
* Score-based progression
* Designed for quick and replayable sessions
